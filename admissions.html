Admission Requirements

  • Completed primary school certificate or equivalent
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Passport size photograph
  • Academic transcripts (last 2 years)
  • Recommendation letters (optional but preferred)

Admission Process

  • Fill out the online application form below
  • Submit required documents
  • Attend entrance examination (dates communicated via email)
  • Receive admission decision via email
  • Complete fee payment and registration
